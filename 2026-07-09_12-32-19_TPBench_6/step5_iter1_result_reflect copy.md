# error_desc:
The leading asymptotic factor $\frac{\pi}{3}e^{-\pi m/H_I}$ correctly combines the one-pole prefactor from Step 3 with the magnitude of the steepest-descent exponential from Step 4. However, the stated multiplicative correction $\left[1+O\!\left(\frac{m^2a_e}{kH_I}\right)\right]$ for the magnitude is not justified by the preceding action evaluation. The omitted term in Step 4 appears in the phase of $\exp[-2iI]$, not in its modulus, so it does not directly generate a relative correction of that order to $|\beta(k)|$. Any subleading correction to the magnitude would require computing subleading real parts of the action or corrections to the one-pole prefactor, which have not been established.
 # fix_instructions:
Keep the leading result as $$|\beta(k)|\sim \frac{\pi}{3}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\pi m}{H_I}\right),\qquad 0<m\lesssim H_I,\quad \frac{k}{a_eH_I}\to\infty.$$ If discussing the first omitted term from the action, attach it to the complex phase rather than to the magnitude, e.g. $$\beta(k)\sim \frac{\pi}{3}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\pi m}{H_I}\right)\exp\!\left[-\frac{2ik}{a_eH_I}+O\!\left(\frac{m^2a_e}{kH_I}\right)i\right],$$ up to uncomputed prefactor corrections. For the magnitude, write only $$|\beta(k)|=\frac{\pi}{3}e^{-\pi m/H_I}[1+o(1)]$$ unless subleading magnitude corrections are explicitly derived.
