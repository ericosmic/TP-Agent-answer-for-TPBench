# error_desc:
The local expansion $\omega_k'/(2\omega_k)=1/[4(\eta-\tilde\eta)]+O(1)$ and its formal residue $1/4$ are correct because $F=\omega_k^2$ has a simple zero. However, the conclusion that the contour contribution is $2\pi i$ times this residue is not valid. At a simple zero of $F$, $\omega_k\sim\sqrt{F'(\tilde\eta)(\eta-\tilde\eta)}$, so the phase has the nonanalytic behavior $\int_{\tilde\eta}^{\eta}\omega_k d\eta'\sim \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{F'(\tilde\eta)}(\eta-\tilde\eta)^{3/2}$. Thus the complete integrand is not meromorphic at $\tilde\eta$; it has a branch point, and the ordinary residue theorem cannot be applied by treating the exponential as an analytic constant. Consequently, the claimed prefactor $\pi/2$ is generally incorrect. For the standard full steepest-descent/Hankel contour around a simple turning point, the local change of variable $u=-\frac{4i}{3}\sqrt{F'(\tilde\eta)}(\eta-\tilde\eta)^{3/2}$ gives $\frac{1}{4}\frac{d\eta}{\eta-\tilde\eta}=\frac{1}{6}\frac{du}{u}$, producing a magnitude $\pi/3$, subject to the precise contour and Stokes prescription.
 # fix_instructions:
Retain the correct pole expansion, but treat $\tilde\eta$ as a square-root turning point of $\omega_k$, not as an ordinary pole of the full integrand. Write $z=\eta-\tilde\eta$ and expand $$\omega_k(\eta)=\sqrt{F'(\tilde\eta)}\,z^{1/2}+O(z^{3/2}),$$ $$-2i\int_{\tilde\eta}^{\eta}\omega_k d\eta'=-\frac{4i}{3}\sqrt{F'(\tilde\eta)}\,z^{3/2}+O(z^{5/2}).$$ Then set $u=-\frac{4i}{3}\sqrt{F'(\tilde\eta)}z^{3/2}$, for which $$\frac{1}{4}\frac{dz}{z}=\frac{1}{6}\frac{du}{u}.$$ Evaluate the resulting Hankel/steepest-descent contour with the specified branch and Stokes orientation. For the standard full contour, this yields $$|\beta(k)|\approx\frac{\pi}{3}\left|\exp\left[-2i\int_{\eta_e}^{\tilde\eta}\omega_k(\eta')d\eta'\right]\right|,$$ rather than the residue-based $\pi/2$. If a different contour prescription is intended, it must be stated explicitly because it can alter the contour multiplier.
